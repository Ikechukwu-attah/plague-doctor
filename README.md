# Plague Doctor

*Understand what's circulating before you gather.*

Plague Doctor turns the Public Health Agency of Canada's weekly wastewater monitoring (COVID-19, Flu A, Flu B, RSV) into plain-language, dated planning cards for gatherings — and says **"too early to know"** when the data can't say.

> Kiroween 2026 · Costume Contest · Built with [Kiro](https://kiro.dev)

**Status:** Milestone 1 (data ingestion) in progress.

## Not medical advice
Plague Doctor shows community wastewater observations. It does not diagnose, forecast, or estimate anyone's personal risk.

## Data
Source: Public Health Agency of Canada — National Wastewater Monitoring of Pathogens (https://health-infobase.canada.ca/wastewater/). Plague Doctor is not affiliated with or endorsed by PHAC. Licence/attribution wording: _to be confirmed (see docs/BUILD_PLAN.md §6)._
