# Wording safety

All user-facing copy is **informational**. Templates only; no free-form generated health statements.

## Banned phrases / meanings (case-insensitive)
- "safe", "unsafe", "dangerous", "risk-free"
- "you will get sick", "you won't get sick", "X% chance", "probability of infection"
- "will be high/low on {date}", "forecast for your event", "predict(ion)" applied to future activity
- "no infections", "virus-free", "all clear"
- anything implying PHAC endorses Plague Doctor

## Required with every measurement
- Location, virus, PHAC classification, and "week of {date}".
- On event cards: "community wastewater, not your chance of getting sick" and "not conditions on your date" (when the event is in the future).

## Prevention copy
Only from PHAC "Respiratory infectious diseases: How to reduce the spread" (vaccines, stay home when sick, well-fitting mask, ventilation, hand hygiene, cover coughs, clean surfaces), with source link and review date.

## Good / bad
- ✅ "Flu A in Toronto's wastewater was Moderate in the week of Oct 18, 2026, and stable."
- ✅ "It's too early to know what will be circulating on Dec 21."
- ❌ "Toronto is safe for your dinner." ❌ "Flu will peak at Christmas."
